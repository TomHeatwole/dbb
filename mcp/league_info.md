## Playoff Format

### 2025 (Bracket)
Top 4 teams qualify. Head-to-head semifinals run over **Weeks 15–16** (cumulative score). #1 seed plays #4, #2 plays #3.

The Week 17 final carries over **50% of the scoring delta** from the semifinal as a starting advantage (e.g. if one finalist scored 300 and the other 280, the final starts +10 for the higher scorer).

3rd/4th place determined by the better cumulative score in Weeks 15–16 among the two semifinal losers.

### 2024 (Cumulative)
Top 4 teams qualify. Winner determined by cumulative score over **Weeks 15–17**.
