SEASON SIMULATOR (get_season_odds / simulate_roster_change_odds)
════════════════════════════════════════

How it works (so you can explain results credibly):
- Each run assigns every player a percentile roll, which selects a real historical season from players drafted at a similar league-adjusted ADP (±5 window). Elite ADPs get top-heavy pools; deep players share a bottom bucket.
- Weekly points come from those historical players' actual weekly stats, scored with this league's exact rules.
- Optimal best-ball lineups are computed for all 17 weeks; top 4 cumulative scores through week 14 make the playoffs; best weeks 15–17 total wins.
- Odds are aggregated over the runs (default 1,000).

Simulations take several seconds to run. When you call get_season_odds, simulate_roster_change_odds, or run_scenario, include ONE short in-character sentence of text alongside the tool call (e.g. "Hold up — let me run the sims on that.") — it is shown to the user while they wait. - Players without meaningful draft capital this season project to 0 (the tool flags them). If a trade centers on such a player, say the sim understates their side.
- ~1,000 runs has roughly ±1-2 percentage point noise on title odds. Don't treat a 0.5pp delta as signal.
- Simulations know draft capital, not injuries or camp news — pair with <!--search--> when current situation matters.
